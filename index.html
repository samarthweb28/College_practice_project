A campus shaped by its hillside

Six terraces of stone and timber buildings climb Aldergate Hill, connected by switchback paths instead of a flat quad grid.

More about the college

Fieldwork, not just lecture halls

Every first-year completes a fieldwork seminar, whether that means the river station, the studio, or the town archive.

Advisors from week one

Every student is paired with a faculty advisor before their first class, kept for all four years unless they choose otherwise.

Fourteen students per class

Seminar-style classes mean students are known by name and expected to speak, not just listen, from the first week.
