How to Apply

Applicants to Kestrel Hill College submit the Common Application along with a graded writing sample, a school report, and two teacher recommendations. Standardized test scores are optional. The application also asks for a short response describing a place you have observed closely — a nod to the college's fieldwork tradition.

Application Deadlines

November 15

Early Decision deadline

Binding early decision applications are due, with decisions released by December 15.

January 15

Regular Decision deadline

Regular decision applications are due, with decisions released by late March.

March 1

Transfer deadline

Transfer applications are due for fall entry, with rolling decisions through April.

Tuition and Financial Aid

Kestrel Hill College meets full demonstrated financial need for every admitted student, and roughly sixty percent of students receive some form of need-based grant aid. The college also offers work-study positions in the river station, the Observatory Library, and the Founders Dining Hall.

Visit Campus

Prospective students can visit Kestrel Hill College for a guided hillside campus tour, an information session with an admissions counselor, or an overnight stay in a first-year dorm during the academic year. Fall and spring open house weekends include a sample seminar class alongside current students.
