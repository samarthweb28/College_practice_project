Housing on the Hill

Nearly all students at Kestrel Hill College live on campus in one of six residence halls terraced up the hillside, from the first-year Aldergate Hall to the upper-hill Observatory House reserved for juniors and seniors. Each hall has a shared common room with a wood stove and a view of the river valley.

Dining and Food

The Founders Dining Hall sources produce from the college's own hillside garden and from farms across the Aldergate valley. A smaller cafe in the Observatory Library serves coffee and pastries and stays open late during exam weeks.

Clubs and Organizations

Kestrel Hill College has more than sixty student clubs, including the Aldergate Trail Crew, the campus radio station WKHC, a student-run literary magazine called The Contour, a rock climbing club, and a river conservation group that monitors water quality along the watershed.

WKHC Radio

Student-run campus radio, broadcasting from a studio in the Founders Quad basement.

The Contour

A twice-yearly literary magazine of student poetry, fiction, and essays.

Trail Crew

Maintains the switchback paths and stairways that connect the hillside campus.

Athletics: The Kestrels

Kestrel Hill College fields eleven varsity teams, the Kestrels, competing in the Hillside Athletic Conference, along with a strong intramural program open to all students. The cross country teams train on the hill's switchback trails, and the boathouse on the Aldergate River supports rowing and kayaking.

Traditions

Each October, Kestrel Hill College holds Lantern Night, when first-year students carry lanterns up the hillside path to Aldergate Hall. In spring, the whole campus turns out for River Day, a day of races, food, and music along the Aldergate River.
